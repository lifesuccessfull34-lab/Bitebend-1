Phase 7 — Session History & Bill History

Goal:

Allow restaurant owners to view, search, filter, and re-open historical sessions and bills after payment is completed.

Current situation:

* Sessions can be created.
* Bills can be generated.
* Bills can be sent via WhatsApp.
* Payment screenshots can be verified.
* Sessions can be closed.

Once a session is closed, restaurant owners need historical visibility.

---

PART A — Closed Sessions View

Create a new Dashboard tab:

History

Show:

* Closed Sessions
* Paid Bills
* Cancelled Bills

Each history card should show:

* Table Number
* Bill Number
* Customer Name
* Total Amount
* Payment Status
* Session Status
* Closed Time

---

PART B — Session Details View

When opening a historical session:

Show:

* All orders in the session
* Items ordered
* Quantities
* Totals
* Bill information
* Payment screenshot (if available)
* Verification information

Show:

* Verified By
* Verified At

---

PART C — Search & Filters

Allow searching by:

* Bill Number
* Customer Phone
* Customer Name
* Table Number

Allow filtering by:

* Today
* Yesterday
* Last 7 Days
* Last 30 Days
* Custom Date Range

---

PART D — Bill Reprint

Add:

View Bill

Button

When clicked:

Show the complete generated bill again.

Restaurant staff should be able to review past bills.

Do NOT generate PDF yet.

Use existing bill data.

---

PART E — Resend Bill

Add:

Resend Bill

Button

Rules:

Only available when:

* bill.status = sent
  OR
* bill.status = paid

Use existing WhatsApp Bridge.

Do NOT create a new bill.

Reuse the existing bill.

Track:

resentAt
resentCount

---

PART F — Revenue Summary

At top of History page show:

Today Revenue
This Week Revenue
This Month Revenue

Using only:

paid bills

Cancelled bills must not count.

---

PART G — Pagination

History must support:

* pagination
* sorting
* date filtering

Do not load all historical records at once.

---

Do NOT Implement

* PDF export
* OCR
* AI verification
* Accounting exports

A restaurant owner should be able to:

Open History
→ Search Bill

without accessing the active sessions dashboard.
